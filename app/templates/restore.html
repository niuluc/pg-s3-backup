Restore as a copy

Replace the live database

  1. Takes a fresh backup of the live database first, so you can undo this.
  2. In one transaction, drops every schema of the live database, including tables added after this backup, and replays the backup. If anything fails, nothing changes.

Writes made after {{ts $f.ModTime}} are lost. Stop the app first if it writes a lot. Type {{$t.Database}} to confirm.
